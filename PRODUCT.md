# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Busy individuals and families who want to plan healthy, delicious meals without feeling overwhelmed. They are often pressed for time, juggle grocery shopping, budgeting, and meal preparation, and need a clear, friction-free way to organize their week.

## Product Purpose
WeekBite serves as a calm weekly meal compass. It helps users organize their weekly meal plans, track dietary goals/streaks, manage pantry/grocery lists, and reduce prep-time friction, ultimately saving hours of cooking anxiety and keeping food budgets on track.

## Brand Personality
Calm, organic, and helpful. Warm but clean. A reassuring kitchen companion rather than a demanding fitness tracker.
- **Tone**: Reassuring, grounded, frictionless.
- **Emotional Goals**: Evoke confidence, calm, and culinary enjoyment.

## Anti-references
- High-stress fitness apps with aggressive neon colors, gamification loops, and loud warnings.
- Over-engineered recipe repositories cluttered with ads, pop-ups, and dense walls of text.
- Bland, cold, clinical dashboards that feel like Excel spreadsheets instead of a warm home kitchen.

## Design Principles
1. **Kitchen Calm**: Reduce visual noise and cognitive load. The kitchen is busy enough; the planner should feel like a sanctuary.
2. **Grounded Utility**: Focus on immediate, practical helpfulness (e.g., prep times, prep-ahead indicators, and clear cooking states).
3. **Organic Flow**: Use natural layouts and soft, human touches to make meal planning feel welcoming, not robotic.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Clear text contrast (≥4.5:1 ratio) to ensure readability under dynamic kitchen lighting.
- Support for reduced motion, avoiding distracting or bouncy transitions.
- Large, clear touch targets and highly readable typography for quick scanning while multitasking.
