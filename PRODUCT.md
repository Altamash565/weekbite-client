# WeekBite Product Compass

## 1. Product Purpose
**WeekBite** serves as a calm weekly meal compass. It helps busy individuals and households organize their weekly meals, maintain dietary goals without pressure, automate grocery checklists from planned recipes, and reduce kitchen preparation friction—ultimately eliminating cooking anxiety and keeping food budgets on track.

---

## 2. Target Users
- **Busy Professionals & Couples**: People with tight schedules who want to eat healthy, home-cooked food without spending hours debating *"What's for dinner?"* every night.
- **Families**: Households coordinating varied dietary preferences and aiming to minimize food waste and repetitive grocery trips.
- **Calm Kitchen Enthusiasts**: Users who cook to decompress and want an app that feels like a quiet, reassuring kitchen companion rather than a high-stress tracker.

---

## 3. Brand Personality & Emotional Goals
- **Personality**: Calm, organic, and helpful. Warm but clean. A reassuring kitchen companion rather than a demanding fitness tracker.
- **Tone**: Grounded, supportive, frictionless.
- **Emotional Goals**: Evoke confidence, culinary enjoyment, and mental clarity.

### Anti-References (What WeekBite is NOT)
- ❌ **Aggressive Fitness Trackers**: No neon warnings, calorie shaming, or pushy streak-loss alerts.
- ❌ **Ad-Cluttered Recipe Sites**: No autoplay videos, 10-paragraph life stories before the ingredients, or pop-up takeovers.
- ❌ **Clinical Spreadsheets**: No sterile, gray Excel-like grids. Kitchens are sensory and warm; our UI should reflect that warmth.

---

## 4. Core Features
1. **The Compass (Weekly Planner)**:
   - Visual 7-day overview with breakfast, lunch, and dinner slots.
   - Low-friction meal assignment with drag-and-drop or one-tap filling.
   - Flexible day re-ordering when plans change.
2. **Automated Grocery Checklist**:
   - Aggregates ingredients from all planned meals for the week.
   - Combines quantities and groups items by supermarket aisle (Produce, Dairy, Pantry, etc.).
   - Offline-first resilience for supermarket aisles with poor connectivity.
3. **Recipe Companion**:
   - Clean, minimal step-by-step cooking view with integrated prep timers.
   - Ingredient checklists that can be toggled while cooking.
4. **Dietary Compass & Preferences**:
   - Supports vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, keto, and allergy exclusions.
   - Household portion scaling that automatically recalibrates grocery amounts.

---

## 5. Design Principles
1. **Kitchen Calm**: Reduce visual clutter and cognitive overhead. The cooking space is busy enough; the digital planner must feel like a sanctuary.
2. **Grounded Utility**: Focus on immediate, practical helpfulness (prep times, thaw-ahead indicators, and clear cooking states).
3. **Organic Flow**: Use warm neutral palettes, gentle rounded surfaces, and natural micro-interactions.
4. **Dynamic Kitchen Accessibility**: High contrast ratios (≥4.5:1) for readability under harsh or dim kitchen lighting, with large touch targets for wet or distracted hands.
